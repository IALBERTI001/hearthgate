# city-north-gate v1 (Nov 5 2026)

Brief: Hearthgate's city painting, with the gatehouse on the north side facing the field the waves come from. The user rejected the reused Warden hub painting because its gatehouse faces south, away from the waves (Nov 5 2026). Two images from one layout: restored first (the base), then ruined, painted from it so they line up.

- Seen: the middle of the portrait screen, 720 px wide and about 440 px tall at play; master 1536x1024, the game crops about 44 px off the top and the bottom (magenta lines in the guide).
- Read in half a second: a walled city whose gate faces up, toward the field; a road from the gate to a round plaza; ten buildings around it, three of them elemental (fire, ice, lightning).
- Proportions: gatehouse about 1.6x the wall's height; tower platforms as wide as the wall is tall; each building plot about 190x140 px on the master.
- Attach: art/guides/city-north-gate_layout_v1.png, art/reference/warden-hub-restored.jpg, art/reference/gatehouse-restored-crop.png.

# keyart v1-v2 (Nov 12 2026)

Brief: store cover key art for CrazyGames (rules checked Nov 12 2026: only the title as text, no borders, no icons; they
recommend a main character or illustration over a gameplay screenshot). The Magister is the game's character. The title is
set afterwards in the game's display font (Lilita One), so the top quarter is kept calm. Made in the "Create Fantasy City
Map" chat so the city, villagers and Magister sheets are the style reference. Landscape 1536x1024; a portrait version for
800x1200; the square is cropped from whichever reads best.

# Hearthgate: style guide (art part)

Approved Nov 5 2026 in "Hearthgate — Kickoff Pitch & Style Guide": Hearthgate reuses Warden of Power's look.

## Style sentence (start every art prompt with it)
Painted cartoon fantasy, three-quarter top-down camera, chunky readable shapes with dark coloured outlines, rich elemental colours, warm golden light from the upper left, clean flat play areas with detail kept to the borders.

## Reference board (art/reference)
- warden-hub-restored.jpg: the city painting style (Warden's restored hub).
- warden-outer-seal.jpg: meadow, walls and orb pillars of the Outer Seal kit.
- gatehouse-to-keep.png and gatehouse-restored-crop.png: the gold-domed arched gatehouse the user wants facing the waves (Nov 5 2026).

## Camera and light
- Three-quarter top-down, about 35 degrees; buildings show their front face and roof.
- Warm light from the upper left; soft shadows to the lower right. Dark coloured outlines.

## Layout of the one screen (portrait 720x1280)
Field (enemies come down from the forest) on top, the city's north wall and gatehouse across the middle with four tower platforms built into it, the city below, Upgrade and Restore at the bottom.

## Palette
UI plum #4a2a4f and brass #c9a24a, ink #fff4dc. Fire #ff7a2e, Frost #8fe3ff, Storm #c77dff. Ruin grey stone to warm lit windows when restored.

## Scale table (720 px stage)
- Element tower: about 112 px tall, base about 90 px wide.
- Wall: about 65 px tall; gatehouse about 1.6x the wall height.
- Enemies: slime and beetle 70 to 80 px wide, golem boss 150 px.

## Rules
- One asset, one sheet (Nov 5 2026): every sprite in its own file, split by outline from any shared image.
- A reused background is placed by its focal point on the screen's centre line.

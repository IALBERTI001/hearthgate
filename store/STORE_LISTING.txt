Hearthgate: CrazyGames listing draft (Nov 5 2026)

Title: Hearthgate
Genre: Strategy > Tower Defense (Idle)
Tags: idle, tower defense, defense, upgrade, strategy, city building, clicker, mobile
Devices: mobile, tablet, desktop (portrait)

Short description:
Hold the gate, and the city rebuilds itself behind you. Your towers fight every wave on their own. Tap Upgrade to make them stronger and Restore to bring a ruined city back to life. Every building you restore changes the fight.

Description:
Hearthgate is an idle defense game about a city coming back to life. Waves of slimes, beetles and stone golems pour out of the forest toward your gate, and the four towers on the wall fight them by themselves.

Restore the Forge and your towers shoot fire. Restore the Frost Spire and they slow. Restore the Storm Bell and lightning chains between enemies. Mix them for combos: SHATTER a frozen enemy with fire, chain lightning through a slowed crowd, set the whole wave burning.

Ten buildings to raise from the ruins, each with its own bonus. Upgrade paths at gate stages 2 and 3. A boss every 10 waves. Restore the whole city, then Ascend for a permanent bonus and build it again, stronger.

The gate keeps earning while you are away, and a daily reward waits every day.

Controls:
Tap or click Upgrade and Restore. On a keyboard: U to upgrade, R to restore, P to pause.
